# PRIMEVEST live-site assessment

Observed 1 November 2026 at `https://vantabrokerage.vercel.app/`. This is a point-in-time, unauthenticated technical and visual review; no account was created, no email was sent, and no deposit or order was attempted. No Vercel project, deployment log, database or provider account access was available. Do not treat this review as a security audit or proof of live trading readiness.

## What was observed

- The site is publicly reachable. The homepage, markets, forex, crypto, registration and login pages returned HTTP 200. An unauthenticated dashboard request redirected to login. This confirms routes load, not that sign-up, email, authenticated flows or back-office operations work end to end.
- CoinGecko discovery returned 250 assets with `stale: false` at the time of checking. These are periodic external USD reference prices, not internal market quotes or executable orders. Provider limits, freshness and availability can change.
- `/api/markets` returned HTTP 200 with `markets: []`. `/api/markets/EUR%2FUSD/candles?interval=5m&limit=48` and `/api/markets/BTC%2FUSDT/quote` returned HTTP 404 with “Market not found.” Consequently the homepage market overview was empty and chart data was unavailable. Most likely the deployed database contains no enabled instrument definitions or points to the wrong/empty environment, but the cause cannot be verified without database and deployment access.
- The deployed homepage showed a hard-coded count of 44 seeded instruments and a green “Live” label even when market data was absent. Those claims were not consistent with the observed endpoint. The repository contains a correction to report the actual available count and show an unavailable state; it takes effect only after the corrected commit is deployed.
- There is no HTML video on the homepage. A decorative Three.js/WebGL animation is present; it is not video footage and does not show people trading. The requested human trading imagery is not on the homepage. The previously generated studio image is conceptual artwork, not footage of real trades.
- Inter and IBM Plex Mono font faces loaded in the browser during inspection; the concern is the visual direction and hierarchy, not evidence that those font files failed. The design remains text/card-heavy, lacks a strong editorial trading visual and has an empty hero chart. Desktop and mobile had no page-level horizontal overflow in the sampled browser session.

## What is required next

1. **Contain the current deployment.** Restrict the site to private review; it is already publicly reachable and must not be presented as a functioning real-money brokerage. Avoid real deposits and sensitive customer data. Confirm deployment protection and use separate disposable infrastructure.
2. **Investigate the empty market database.** Check the active Vercel deployment/branch, `DATABASE_URL`, migrations and enabled `TradingPair` records; verify server logs and external-provider connectivity. After confirming a disposable review database and its backup, the review-only migration and `db:seed-review` steps in `docs/VERCEL-REVIEW-DEPLOYMENT.md` can initialize reference instrument definitions. Never run the development seed or migration commands blindly against customer data. Instrument definitions do not constitute live trading, and 250 CoinGecko assets do not automatically become tradeable.
3. **Redesign the homepage.** Create a cohesive hero, stronger hierarchy and typography, real visual storytelling, responsive layouts, graceful loading and reduced-motion alternatives. Source licensed footage of consenting human traders if actual footage is required; alternatively use clearly labelled conceptual artwork. A video asset has not been produced or integrated. Neither imagery nor a WebGL animation is evidence of live trading.
4. **Build real operation.** Decide product scope and jurisdictions. For the selected internal-matching model, design and implement an independently tested matching engine and durable order book, accurate balances, execution events, settlement, recovery, liquidity and exchange/broker connectivity as needed. External reference prices may differ from PRIMEVEST's own orders and are not guarantees of fill prices.
5. **Funding, custody and operations.** Integrate authorized custody and fiat/crypto payment rails; implement verified deposits, reconciliation, customer-funds controls, withdrawals and operational monitoring. The current customer deposit endpoint and live execution remain disabled in the repository. Legacy simulated features still exist elsewhere and should not be confused with real activity.
6. **Email and deployment.** Confirm `APP_URL`, a verified sending domain and configured email provider; conduct a real test to a controlled mailbox. Branded templates exist in the repository, but delivery has not been independently verified for this deployment. Review database pooling, provider rate limits, background processing, secrets, access controls, logging, and test/production separation.
7. **Before real customers or money.** Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal/licensing review and independent security, privacy and financial-control audits; review KYC/AML/sanctions, market-data licensing, incident response and terms. Brand-name clearance also remains open.
