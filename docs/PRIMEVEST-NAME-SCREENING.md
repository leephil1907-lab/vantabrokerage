# PRIMEVEST — preliminary name and domain screening

**Checked:** 31 October 2026. **Scope:** open-web screening and a limited domain lookup. This is not a trademark clearance, a legal opinion, a company-name reservation, or a domain purchase. No jurisdiction was specified; Nigeria is noted as a possible starting point, not assumed to be the only market.

## Recommendation

**Do not commit to PRIMEVEST for a public financial-services launch yet.** Similar names are already used online by businesses presenting forex/crypto or investment services, so customer confusion and brand distinctiveness are substantial concerns. This is a commercial risk indication, not a legal conclusion of infringement. The current PRIMEVEST website is a private demonstration; no brand name or logo has been cleared.

## What remains

1. Confirm launch jurisdictions, intended legal entity and the exact services and products offered.
2. Have qualified trademark counsel search word marks and the logo, exact names and confusingly similar marks, relevant goods/services classes and unregistered use in each jurisdiction. The Canadian record above is only one historical result.
3. If Nigeria is in scope, conduct an official IPO availability search (including class 36 and any other appropriate classes) and a CAC name search/reservation. Company registration does not itself confer a financial-services licence or replace trademark clearance.
4. Check desired domains directly with an accredited registrar immediately before purchase. Do not treat a website check as proof that a domain is unregistered or legally safe to use.
5. Separately obtain counsel's advice on financial-services licensing and compliance. Name clearance alone does not make the brokerage deployment-ready.
