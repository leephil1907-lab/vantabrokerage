# Multi-asset workspace and session clocks

`/dashboard/trade` combines configured FOREX, CFD, SPOT and FUTURES categories in one searchable, keyboard-operable workspace. Short-expiry remains a separate workflow, linked from the desk. Actual instruments come from the configured market catalogue, not a hard-coded claim of executable markets. If it is empty, the workspace offers a small clearly identified selection of third-party reference charts instead, without creating records or implying order placement is available. Charts with no explicit TradingView mapping can use the configured provider chart instead. TradingView reference symbols can represent a related index or instrument rather than the configured CFD, and never establish an execution price. Kraken market depth is external and only appears for supported exact pairs.

The public homepage and multi-asset desk display indicative FX-centre windows (Sydney 08–17, Tokyo 09–18, London 08–17, New York 08–17, local weekdays). `Intl` uses each centre's IANA timezone, including regional daylight-saving transitions. These conventions are illustrative and not a venue or product calendar: holidays, exceptions, instrument-specific hours, execution availability and maintenance are not calculated. Crypto generally operates around the clock but venue outages and pauses still apply. For additional context, the guide lists the [NYSE core session](https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars) and [London Stock Exchange main market hours](https://www.londonstockexchange.com/personal-investing/faqs). These are cash equity examples, not trading hours for any PRIMEVEST instrument. Commodity and futures contracts have venue-specific schedules. Illustrative FX window conventions: [Deriv forex session guide](https://traders-academy.deriv.com/trading-guides/forex-session-times-and-overlaps).

Positions, orders and fills remain authenticated account data; a failed account refresh is labelled and does not pretend cached records are current. Neither the third-party chart, external book, clock nor this workspace implements a live matching engine, custody, liquidity, funding or settlement. Existing fail-closed protections remain in place. No live customer data is seeded or modified by this change.
